---
name: adneuron-launch
description: Review and activate an existing campaign only after explicit approval
---
Use current AdNeuron remote MCP schemas. Resolve the exact authorized workspace, account and campaign. Inspect current campaign, ad set or ad group, ad status, budgets, currency, targeting, destination, schedule, and applicable readiness information. Explain blockers and unavailable checks. Show the exact objects to activate and spending implications. Wait for explicit approval of this activation; creation approval alone does not authorize launch. Respect any server-required in-app approval for recommended changes. Execute only the approved supported operation, then verify resulting status and distinguish activation from actual delivery or platform review. Do not auto-retry writes or bypass approval checks.
